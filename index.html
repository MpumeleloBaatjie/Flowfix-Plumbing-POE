Plumbing Tips

Plumbing Emergency? Do This First

  1. Turn off the main water valve to stop the flow.
  2. If water is near plugs or the electrical board, keep clear and switch the power off only if it is safe to reach.
  3. Move valuables and furniture away from the water.
  4. Call us straight away on 012 345 6789 - emergency callouts are available at any time.
