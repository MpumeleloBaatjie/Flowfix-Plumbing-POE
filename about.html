About FlowFix Plumbing Services

Our Story

FlowFix started in 2019 when Banele Ben Baatjie began fixing plumbing problems for people in his own neighbourhood. Word got around, and after a while it turned into a proper business serving homes and small businesses around Pretoria. We now offer various plumbing services to our community at affordable prices.

What We Believe In

Show up on time, tell the customer the truth about the problem, and do not overcharge. That is really the whole plan. We believe that as a community we should strive to help one another and not exploit our people. We believe that all residents deserve a well maintained, hygienic environment.
